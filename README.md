Rules:
Draft Your Squad: Click Roll All 6 to get 6 fully evolved Kanto Pokémon.
Use your 1 free Re-Roll if you don't like the lineup, then hit Ready for Battle.
Turn Options: On your turn, choose one action:Fast Attack: Deals light damage and grants +1 Energy.
Charged Attack: Unlocks at 3/3 Energy to deal massive burst damage (resets energy to 0).
Switch Pokémon: Tap any healthy Pokémon on your side bench to tag them into battle (ends your turn).
Switching Rules & Cooldowns:Voluntary Swap: Puts switching on a 5-move cooldown. You must make 5 attacks before you can voluntarily swap again.
Fainted Swap: If your active Pokémon reaches 0 HP, you get a free immediate swap from your bench without triggering the cooldown.
Mega Evolution:You get 1 Mega Evolution per match.If your active Pokémon can Mega Evolve, the pink Mega Evolve button will appear. Mega Evolving is a free action (you can still attack on the same turn) and grants +40% Attack, +30% HP, boosted move damage, and 25% damage reduction.
For Pokémon with two forms, pick your preferred form/typing from the pop-up menu.Type Advantage Matters: Matchups deal 2x damage for Super Effective hits, 0.5x for Not Very Effective hits, and 0 for Immunities.
Win the Match: Knock out all 6 of your opponent's Pokémon to win the Arena.
