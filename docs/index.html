📜 How to Play & Game Rules
  • Draft Your Squad: Each player is presented with choices of 2 Pokémon pairs. The Pokémon you pick joins your squad, while the unpicked Pokémon goes to your opponent's squad.
  • Turn Options: On your turn, choose one action:
    • Fast Attack: Deals light damage and grants +1 Energy (capped at a maximum of 3/3 charges).
    • Charged Attack: Unlocks at 3/3 Energy to deal massive burst damage (resets energy to 0).
    • Switch Pokémon: Tap any healthy Pokémon on your bench to tag them into battle (ends your turn).
  • Turn Timer: You have 10 seconds to make a move on your turn. If time runs out without any action, your turn is skipped completely (no attack performed, no energy gained) and control passes to your opponent.
  • Switching Rules & Cooldowns:
    • Voluntary Swap: Puts switching on a 5-move cooldown. You must make 5 attacks before you can voluntarily swap again.
    • Fainted Swap: If your active Pokémon reaches 0 HP, you get a free instant replacement with no cooldown restrictions. You have 5 seconds to choose a replacement; if you don't choose, a random healthy Pokémon is automatically sent out and your turn passes to the opponent.
  • Mega Evolution: You get 1 Mega Evolution per match. If your active Pokémon can Mega Evolve, the pink Mega Evolve button will appear. Mega Evolving consumes your turn (you cannot attack on the same turn) and grants +40% Attack, +30% HP, boosted move damage, and 25% damage reduction. For Pokémon with two forms, pick your preferred form/typing from the pop-up menu.
  • Type Advantage Matters: Matchups deal 2x damage for Super Effective hits, 0.5x for Not Very Effective hits, and 0.2x for Immunities.
  • Win the Match: Knock out all 6 of your opponent's Pokémon to win the Arena.
