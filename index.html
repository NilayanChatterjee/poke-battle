📜 How to Play & Game Rules
  • Draft Your Squad: Each player is presented with 3 choices of 2 Pokémon pairs. The Pokémon you pick joins your squad, while the unpicked Pokémon goes to your opponent's squad.
  • Turn Options: On your turn, choose one action:
    • Fast Attack: Deals light damage and grants +1 Energy.
    • Charged Attack: Unlocks at 3/3 Energy to deal massive burst damage (resets energy to 0).
    • Switch Pokémon: Tap any healthy Pokémon on your bench to tag them into battle (ends your turn).
  • Switching Rules & Cooldowns:
    • Voluntary Swap: Puts switching on a 5-move cooldown. You must make 5 attacks before you can voluntarily swap again.
    • Fainted Swap: If your active Pokémon reaches 0 HP, you get 5 seconds to choose a replacement from your bench before a random one is automatically picked.
  • Mega Evolution: You get 1 Mega Evolution per match. If your active Pokémon can Mega Evolve, the pink Mega Evolve button will appear. Mega Evolving is a free action (you can still attack on the same turn) and grants +40% Attack, +30% HP, boosted move damage, and 25% damage reduction. For Pokémon with two forms, pick your preferred form/typing from the pop-up menu.
  • Type Advantage Matters: Matchups deal 2x damage for Super Effective hits, 0.5x for Not Very Effective hits, and 0 for Immunities.
  • Win the Match: Knock out all 6 of your opponent's Pokémon to win the Arena.
