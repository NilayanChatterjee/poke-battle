📜 How to Play & Game Rules
  • Drafting: Choose 1 of 2 Pokémon pairs each round until you have 6. Unchosen goes to your rival.
  • Actions: Select Fast Attack (+1 Energy), Charged Attack (needs 3 Energy), or switch bench members.
  • Faint Timer: When your Pokémon fainted, you have 5 seconds to pick a sub or an auto-sub happens.
